Privacy notice

How Forj handles your data.

Last updated: May 2026

Forj is a Nordic practice operated by Jacob Ahmid in Gothenburg, Sweden. This notice describes what personal data is collected through forj.se, how it's used, and your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What data is collected

Forj collects only the personal data you actively provide through the contact form or by emailing directly:

  • Your name
  • Your company name
  • Your email address
  • The message you choose to send

The site does not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising cookies, or third-party trackers. No data is collected passively from visitors who don't submit the form.

How your data is used

Submitted data is used solely to respond to your inquiry and, where relevant, to discuss potential engagements. Your data is not shared with third parties, sold, or used for marketing communications.

Where your data is stored

Form submissions are processed by Netlify, Inc., the hosting provider for forj.se, and a copy is delivered to jacob@forj.se. Netlify acts as a data processor under GDPR Article 28. Their privacy practices are described at netlify.com/privacy.

Email correspondence is hosted by One.com, the email provider for forj.se. Their practices are described at one.com/privacy-policy.

How long data is kept

Inquiry data is retained for up to 24 months from the last interaction, after which it is deleted. If an engagement begins, relevant business correspondence is retained for the duration of the engagement and for the period required by Swedish accounting law (typically 7 years).

Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data Forj holds about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to processing or request restriction of processing
  • Request your data in a portable format
  • Lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY)

To exercise any of these rights, email jacob@forj.se. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as Forj's practices evolve. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
